    <h2>I like understanding how things work—and finding ways to make them better.</h2>
    <p>
      My interests sit at the intersection of software engineering and
      creative development. I enjoy working through the logic behind a
      system just as much as thinking about how someone will interact with it.
    </p>
    <p>

      carefully about the experience a finished project creates.
