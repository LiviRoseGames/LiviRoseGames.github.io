## About My Approach

I'm interested in the intersection of technology, creativity, and user experience.

My background spans computer science, game development, digital illustration, and education. Each has influenced the way I approach software development.

Studying art has helped me understand visual communication and collaborate with artists. Working in education has taught me to consider how people with different levels of experience interact with the tools and systems I create.

Whether I'm developing a gameplay mechanic or building a desktop application, I enjoy figuring out how the individual pieces fit together and finding ways to make the final experience feel intuitive.

I especially enjoy collaborative projects where different creative and technical perspectives come together to build something that none of us would have made alone.
